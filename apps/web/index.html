How to Play

🎲

1. Roll

Seat 1 begins. On your turn, roll two dice and move your pawn one space at a time through Barkley Ville.

🐾

2. Train

Special spaces can award Paw Tokens, advance your Competition Track, trigger a Vet Check, or draw a public Trainer Card.

🏆

3. Finish

The first trainer to reach the Finish podium wins K9 Blitz Digital Rules v1.0.

Rule provenance

The physical reference establishes the Barkley Ville board, two dice, five visible pawn colors, Trainer Cards, dog profiles including Max and Luna, tokens, named action spaces, and the K9 Competition Track. Where the available materials do not establish an exact rule or component detail, the game owner has authorized a versioned digital rule so the game can be complete and playable. Those synthesized rules are labeled owner-authorized rather than source-transcribed.
