How to Play

🎲

1. Roll

On your turn, roll two dice. Your pawn hops one space at a time through Barkley Ville.

🐾

2. Train

Special spaces can award Paw Tokens, advance your Competition Track, trigger a Vet Check, or draw a Trainer Card.

🏆

3. Finish

The first trainer to reach the Finish podium wins this digital demo ruleset.

Rule-fidelity note

The board photograph establishes the visual board, dice, pawns, Trainer Cards, dog profiles, tokens, named action spaces, and K9 Competition Track. Exact physical-game card effects, movement rules, token meanings, competition requirements, and victory conditions are not yet present in the repository. This launch keeps those assumptions isolated in a clearly versioned demo ruleset so authoritative rules can replace them without redesigning the UI.
